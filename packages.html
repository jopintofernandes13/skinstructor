02

Powder Skiing Lessons & Coaching

Learn to move with the snow and become more confident in deeper conditions.

Powder skiing is different from skiing on groomed snow. The aim isn't simply to ski differently — it's to understand how balance, pressure, rhythm and movement change when the snow gets deeper.

We'll work with the conditions of the day and focus on helping you feel more comfortable, controlled and confident in powder. Whether you're experiencing Japanese powder for the first time or looking to improve your technique, the session is adapted to your current ability.

FOCUS

Powder technique
Balance & pressure control
Rhythm and flow
Line choice
Snow awareness


SUITABLE FOR

Intermediate to advanced skiers

03

Off-Piste Resort Guiding

Explore the terrain beyond the groomed runs without leaving the resort.

Niseko offers far more than its marked pistes. With the right conditions and local knowledge, there are plenty of opportunities to explore trees, powder, natural features and more challenging terrain within the resort.

We'll choose terrain according to your experience, confidence and the conditions on the day, making the most of what the mountain has to offer while keeping the experience appropriate for the group.

The goal isn't simply to find the steepest or deepest snow. It's about finding the right terrain for you, making good decisions and enjoying the mountain.

FOCUS

Off-piste resort terrain

Terrain selection
Local mountain knowledge
Adapting to conditions


SUITABLE FOR

Intermediate to advanced skiers with appropriate off-piste experience

04

Niseko Backcountry Guiding

Go beyond the resort and explore Niseko's backcountry.

For experienced skiers looking to venture beyond the resort boundaries, Niseko offers a completely different experience. Backcountry days can take you into quieter terrain, deeper snow and a more natural mountain environment.

The route and terrain will depend on your experience, the weather, snowpack, visibility and conditions on the day. The focus is on making appropriate decisions, choosing terrain that suits the group and enjoying the best possible experience in the mountains.

05

Hokkaido Ski Days

Go beyond Niseko and discover more of Hokkaido.

Hokkaido has an incredible variety of mountains and ski areas, and sometimes the best ski day isn't the one spent at your home resort.

A private Hokkaido ski day gives us the flexibility to choose where to go based on the weather, snowfall and what you're looking for. We can explore a different resort, chase better conditions or simply experience another part of the region.

The day is planned around you and the mountain, rather than following a fixed itinerary.
